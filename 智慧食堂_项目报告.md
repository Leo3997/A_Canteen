## 1. Executive Summary

The Smart Canteen System is a next-generation food service solution powered by advanced Computer Vision and Artificial Intelligence. By integrating the speed of **YOLOv8** with the precision of Meta's **SAM (Segment Anything Model)**, the system solves two critical problems in the catering industry: **inefficient settlement** and **immeasurable food waste**.

This report details the system's technical infrastructure, core capabilities, and the value it delivers to both canteen operators and diners.

## 2. Core Vision

> **"See. Recognize. Analyze."**

Traditional canteens rely on manual cashiering or RFID-chip bowls (which are expensive and require special dishware). Our system eliminates these barriers. It uses a standard RGB camera to "see" the food tray, identify dishes instantly, and even quantify how much food is left after a meal.

## 3. Technical Architecture

The system operates on a robust "Edge-Cloud" architecture designed for low latency and high accuracy.

### 3.1 Hardware Layer

- **Image Acquisition**: Standard 1080p Webcams mounted above the checkout or recycling zones.
- **Processing Unit**: Local Edge Servers (GPU-accelerated) for real-time inference.

### 3.2 AI Core (The "Dual-Brain" Engine)

The heart of the system uses a two-stage pipeline:

1.  **Detection (Fast)**: `YOLOv8` quickly locates food items and assigns labels (e.g., "Rice", "Pork", "Broccoli"). It handles the "What is this?" question.
2.  **Segmentation (Precise)**: `SAM` (Segment Anything Model) refines these detections into pixel-perfect masks. It handles the "Where exactly is it?" question, enabling area calculations.

### 3.3 Application Layer

- **Backend**: Python (FastAPI/Flask) orchestrating the AI models and database interactions.
- **Frontend**: Modern React-based Dashboard for real-time analytics.

## 4. Key Features

### 4.1 Intelligent Recognition & Tray Calibration

![Processing Workflow](file:///C:/Users/17646/.gemini/antigravity/brain/fbce48c2-4294-419e-bb50-e81b12d80d03/food_analysis_process_1769577897786.png)

Real-world usage involves chaotic tray placements. Our `calibrate_plate` module uses perspective transformation algorithms to:

1.  Detect the four corners of a tray.
2.  Warp/Straighten the image to a standard template.
3.  Match food items to specific grid slots (e.g., "Main Dish Slot", "Side Dish Slot").

### 4.2 Leftover Analysis (Waste Quantification)

Unlike traditional systems that only record sales, our system records _consumption_.

- **Mechanism**: By comparing the initial food area (100% full) with the leftover area (e.g., 30% pixels remaining), we calculate a precise **Waste Ratio**.
- **Impact**: This granular data helps canteens optimize portion sizes and menu planning, directly reducing food waste.
